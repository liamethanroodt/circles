
# Circles

A family-focused social media platform where you share moments with the people who matter most. Unlike traditional social media, Circles lets you organize your life into separate circles (Primary Family, Extended Family, Close Friends, etc.) and share content exclusively within each circle.

## Core Concept

- **Circle-based sharing**: Join multiple circles, post to specific circles
- **Focused feed**: View posts only from the circle you're currently viewing
- **Privacy first**: Content stays within your chosen circles
- **Simple & clean**: Instagram-style interface without the noise

## Stack
- Built with [.NET Aspire](https://aspire.dev/) and [React](https://react.dev/)
